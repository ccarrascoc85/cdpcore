First-time setup
This is a fresh CDPcore install. Choose how to protect the /system management pages. You can change this later from /system/admin.
PIN-protected
A 6-digit PIN guards the management pages. Recommended for shared or community spaces, or when the appliance is exposed to untrusted networks.
LAN-Trust
No PIN required. Anyone on the local network can manage this appliance. Home use only — suitable when the LAN is already private and trusted.
The setup endpoint is one-shot. Once either option is saved, this screen will no longer appear.
